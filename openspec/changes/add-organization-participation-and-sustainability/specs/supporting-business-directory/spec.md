## Purpose
Give supporting businesses reviewed, paid visibility while keeping commercial promotion distinct from beneficiary centers, verified services and earned impact.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Distinct Business Profiles
Supporting-business profiles SHALL have stable IDs, business category codes, display name, localized description, approved public contact and public service area. Business categories SHALL be separate from Kindness Center categories. A profile SHALL not become a care-service location, intake center or beneficiary center merely by registering or paying.

#### Scenario: Pharmacy buys a listing
- **WHEN** the commercial profile becomes eligible for display
- **THEN** it appears as a supporting business and does not accept medicine referrals without the separate center/service approval

### Requirement: Authorized Reviewed Claims
Only an authorized organization representative SHALL edit its business profile. Public helping statements SHALL identify evidence scope and dates, distinguishing independently validated activity, self-declared initiatives and platform sponsorship. Unsupported claims SHALL remain unpublished or explicitly unverified. Review SHALL not invent a government endorsement, quality guarantee or tax status.

#### Scenario: Business claims it delivered one thousand meals
- **WHEN** its evidence supports only a planned campaign
- **THEN** the profile cannot present that target as completed validated delivery

### Requirement: Explicit Paid Visibility
Publication SHALL require both approved profile content and an active paid listing term. The directory SHALL disclose commercial inclusion and any sponsored placement order. Payment SHALL not alter organic center/need ranking, verification status or case routing. Expiry SHALL remove commercial visibility without deleting the underlying organization or historical evidence.

#### Scenario: Listing term ends
- **WHEN** no active renewal exists
- **THEN** the commercial entry is no longer served and the organization retains its contribution history and account access

### Requirement: Independent Search and Map Layer
Visitors SHALL browse supporting businesses by category and public area using a dedicated directory or explicitly selected map layer. The default assistance map SHALL continue to show service-qualified centers under existing discovery rules. Business locations SHALL use the same provider-independent geographic contracts without sharing private organization or employee locations.

#### Scenario: Person searches for the nearest shelter
- **WHEN** a paid business is nearer than a suitable shelter
- **THEN** sponsorship does not make that business a shelter result or displace the qualified assistance result

### Requirement: Correctable Accessible Profiles
Web and native business views SHALL show localized business identity, sponsorship disclosure, evidence labels and safe contact actions. Visitors SHALL have a private report route; a report alone SHALL not establish wrongdoing. Corrections, suspension and appeal decisions SHALL be recorded. Removed commercial content SHALL not persist as active through stale caches.

#### Scenario: Business changes its public phone
- **WHEN** the authorized change is accepted under review rules
- **THEN** the public revision updates across clients while private representative contacts remain undisclosed
