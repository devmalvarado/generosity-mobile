## Why

Center directory fields currently exclude two requested capabilities: the expanded
category list and routing usable food surplus to participating centers. A meal
train coordinates planned meals; it does not cover time-sensitive surplus rescue.

## What Changes

- Plan a versioned, localized category taxonomy covering the user's requested center types without implying operational capacity.
- Plan explicit food-surplus offers, authorized center acceptance, safe pickup/receipt tracking, expiry and privacy boundaries.
- Retain the existing directory, center portal, backend identity and native map adapters.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `center-category-taxonomy`: localized multi-category center classification and migration.
- `food-surplus-rescue`: consented time-bounded surplus matching and receipt.

### Modified Capabilities

None; integration details remain in the active foundation and directory changes.

## Impact

PHP import/catalog and staff permissions, KMP models/search, Android/iOS labels and
mobile-first center portal. This is planning only. It does not activate food
handling services, payments, beneficiary records or official verification.
