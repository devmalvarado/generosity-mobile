## Context and decisions

Use stable versioned category codes with Spanish/English resources; preserve old
codes and import compatibility until an explicit migration maps each center.
Classification is multi-valued and distinct from services/capacity/admission rules.
Blood donation must not silently become organ/tissue donation. Shelter locations
may require a public referral point instead of a sensitive residential address.

Food offers record quantities, units, source, preparation/collection timing,
allergen/storage declarations and a pickup deadline under a reviewed operating
protocol. Do not invent universal temperature limits or certify safety from a
form. Students/schools can offer food only through the permissions and operating
protocol assigned to their organization. Expired or rejected offers are not shown
as available; allocation and receipt are transactional, versioned and idempotent.

Reuse account-bound writes, center assignments, portal and public map/list. Public
views expose participating services and availability, never recipient identities.
A center's category alone cannot mean it accepts every food type or medicine.
No donation/point/verified-impact claim follows from an offer or reservation alone.

## Open decisions

Local food-handling protocol, responsible operators, permitted food/source types,
service areas, handling/storage declarations and retention must be approved before
activation. SMS verification for group/event creators follows the foundation;
food-receiver access does not acquire a blanket phone/app requirement.
