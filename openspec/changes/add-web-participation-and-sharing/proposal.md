## Why

Installing an app must not be a prerequisite to help. Shared opportunities need
to lead directly to usable participation, including people assisted by a
coordinator, rather than terminate at a marketing page or app-store prompt.

## What Changes

- Extend the canonical mobile-first portal into a public participation web app, with optional PWA installation and the same accounts, capacity and history as native clients.
- Add public links, previews and QR codes for needs, campaigns, event occurrences and safe meal opportunities, retaining usable browser destinations.
- Add consented assisted participation without fabricated accounts, with shared capacity and a controlled later account-claim process.
- Add consented outcome updates, collective progress and privacy-conscious conversion measurement. Sharing and installs never earn helping points.
- Preserve English/Spanish, browser-language default, accessibility and low-bandwidth access; no requirement for push, precise location or installation to participate.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `public-web-participation`: Browser discovery, authenticated helping, owned history and optional install/offline enhancement.
- `opportunity-invitations`: Stable opportunity links, safe previews/QR codes and voluntary invitations across browser/native entry.
- `assisted-participation`: Authorized coordinator entry, consent, non-account participant records, shared capacity and verified account association.
- `shared-impact-updates`: Attributed collective outcomes, opt-in updates, safe sharing and aggregate funnel measurement.

### Modified Capabilities

No synchronized baseline capability changes. Existing `center-sharing` remains
the center-link contract; the new invitation capability covers other public
opportunities. The assisted path explicitly extends event subject identity:
the acting coordinator must authenticate, but the participant need not own an
account. The original self-service authentication requirements remain intact.

## Impact

- Depends on the foundation backend/authentication/portal and on the reference and community plans for events, meals, inbox and authoritative activity. None is assumed implemented.
- Reuses one backend and canonical web project; native KMP clients gain invitation/association/update contracts, not a second business-rule implementation.
- Adds browser participation, durable assisted subject IDs, share metadata and sanitized public outcome projections. Protected household case workflows remain separate.
- Does not activate payments, send messages, create users or publish campaigns during this planning work. Provider-backed monetary participation remains separately gated.
- Actual discovery-to-completed-help outcomes are measured without promising viral growth or using contact harvesting, automatic posts or referral rewards.
